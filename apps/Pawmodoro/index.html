Pawmodoro

Focus with a friend by your side

Android · Currently in development

About

Pawmodoro is a cozy productivity app for Android that pairs everything you need to get things done with an animal companion who keeps you company along the way. Work in focused sessions, build good habits, and keep your tasks and notes in one place, all while your pet cheers you on.

Get Things Done

Pomodoro Timer

Break your work into focused sessions with short rests in between, and let your companion keep you company while the timer runs.

Habit Tracker

Build routines that stick by tracking the habits that matter to you, one day at a time.

To-Do List

Capture what needs doing and check it off as you go, so you always know what to focus on next.

Notes

Jot down ideas, plans and reminders without leaving the app.

Meet Your Companion

Every Pawmodoro user has an animal companion to work alongside. Make your pet your own and create a space that feels like yours.

Dress Them Up

Give your pet a style with a range of accessories.

Pick a Seat

Change the chair your companion sits in while you work.

Set the Scene

Switch up the background to match your mood.

The Store

Browse the in-app store to personalize your companion and their space. You can purchase:

Pet Accessories

Outfits and extras to dress up your pet.

Pet Chairs

New seats for your companion to relax in.

Backgrounds

Fresh scenery for your focus sessions.
