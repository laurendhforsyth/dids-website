Privacy Policy for Pawmodoro

Effective Date: Nov. 1, 2026
Developer: Lauren Forsyth / Drowning In Dreams Studios

This Privacy Policy describes how Pawmodoro ("we," "our," or "us") handles your data. We built this application with privacy as a foundational principle.

1. Data Access, Collection, and Use

Pawmodoro does not access, collect, use, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

This includes, but is not limited to, personally identifiable information, financial data, authentication information, contacts, device location, SMS/call-related data, health data, or camera/microphone access. Because we do not collect any user data, we do not sell or exchange any data to third parties.

2. Third-Party Code and SDKs

Pawmodoro is built using modern development frameworks (such as Flutter), which may include standard third-party libraries. We strictly ensure that any third-party code integrated into our application complies with Google Play Developer Program policies. We do not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect, transmit, or sell personal and sensitive user data.

3. Persistent Identifiers and App Set ID

We do not collect or link persistent device identifiers (such as IMEI, IMSI, or SIM Serial Numbers) to any personal data. If Android’s App Set ID is utilized strictly for essential, non-advertising analytics or crash reporting, it is never used for ads personalization, ad measurement, or connected to any personally identifiable information or Android Advertising IDs.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Because Pawmodoro does not require you to create an account and does not collect any personal data, we have a strict zero-retention policy. There is no user data stored on our servers to delete. If you wish to stop using the app, simply uninstall it from your device, and no trace of your usage will remain with us. If you wish to swap your data to a different device, use the Export backup in the settings on your old device and send the data to yourself. On the new device, use 'Import backup' in the settings to import the file to your new phone that contains your data.

5. Global Privacy Frameworks (EU, UK, Switzerland)

We process absolutely no personal information originating from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland. By collecting zero personal data, we natively maintain compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other global privacy laws.

6. Children's Privacy

Our zero-data-collection practices ensure full compliance with the Google Play Families policy. We do not collect any personal information from children or use unapproved SDKs for child-directed services.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If any future updates require the collection of data to enable new features, we will provide a prominent in-app disclosure, update this policy to reflect the changes, and request your affirmative consent before any data is collected.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions, inquiries, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Email: drowningindreamsstudios@gmail.com
Website: drowningindreamsstudios.com
